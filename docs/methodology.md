# Statistical processing methodology

The unit of analysis is the published population count for one reference date, territorial unit, education category and marginal breakdown. The column identifiers A and 1–7 anchor the multilevel header. The title supplies the date and can begin in A or B. The source and confidentiality notes close the statistical area. Merged cells, populated coordinates and all worksheet names are inventoried.

Text matching uses NFC and folds whitespace, including non-breaking spaces. Leading indentation is measured before cleaning. Category identity combines raw label, expected indentation and active parent. University is a heading with no facts. Repeated fields have distinct parent-specific IDs. Date, scope and crosswalk references are reviewed configuration. IDs are internal. No mapping to ISCED or official geographic codes is asserted.

Sex and residence totals are two separate marginal distributions. G and H each represent a separate territorial indicator. Their placeholders on every education row are retained in the cell ledger with structural disposition. Applicable dashes remain unresolved. Asterisk cells remain confidential and receive null counts. No arithmetic comparison runs when a required term is nonnumeric.

The finite aggregation checks apply to four university levels that each list six distinct fields and to secondary, upper secondary and high school, whose printed child lists include all component categories for the configured comparison. This interpretation is based on the source’s category structure, the residual “Alt tip” where applicable, and direct reconciliation of complete published cells in this release. University has no numeric heading, and the territorial total has a mix of top-level categories and heading branches. These two nodes are excluded from parent arithmetic. Review the configured exhaustive branches before reusing the mapping with a revised instrument.

The consolidation is byte-reproducible for identical inputs and configuration. Run IDs, timestamps and duration are operational metadata. The output records source provenance and the run records input and output SHA-256 digests.
